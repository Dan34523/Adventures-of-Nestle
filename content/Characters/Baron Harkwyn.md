## Overview
30 year old man, dark hair, goatee. Average build and height. Looks average in every way other than his clothes. First appearance ([[Session 09]]): he slaps [[Reginald Applesby]] across the face at Cuxton Manor, then removes his mask to reveal a massive purple bruise. Furious at the stain on his honour from [[Tuff]]'s allegations against him, he demands a Trial of Nine - which ends with the accusers denounced and [[Reginald Applesby]] declared innocent of all charges.

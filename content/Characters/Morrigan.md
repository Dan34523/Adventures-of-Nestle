## Overview
Arrived at [[Wilheim]] answering word of a lucrative mercenary job, meeting [[Zeal Cross]] on the road. Guards at the gate assumed she was a holy person because of her priestly robes and veil - an assumption that's stuck ever since.

Raised from infancy in the **[[Church of the Martyress]]** after her parents dropped her off as a baby. During a ritual to "promote" her from Acolyte, the church took one of her eyes - she now wears a veiled headband to cover it. The torture and the theft disillusioned her, and she left. 
## Relationship to the Party
Generally the group's moral compass alongside [[Tuff]]; voted to spare [[Harla]] after hearing her story, having recognised her own trauma in it.

## Notes & Secrets
- Cast Detect Magic during the loot-counting in [[The Hag's Mole]] and sensed a stronger magic coming from an object in **[[Althea]]'s bag** - and chose not to mention it. Nobody else knows this yet.
- Accepted Ria Nostakis's blessing (a Bless spell) before the dungeon crawl; [[Althea]] refused hers.
- Has no memory of ever using the term "Angel Eye" for her missing eye, but was visibly uncomfortable when asked.
- Recognised as an apostate by a Church of the Martyress Exquirer at the Ball of the Earth Father and nearly detained; [[Althea]]'s wolf helped cover the escape by urinating on the Exquirer's shoe, and she slipped away into the servant tunnels via a Bag of Holding smuggling job engineered by [[Za'rek]] and [[Tuff]] ([[Session 08]]).
- Recruited [[Tarhun Turneron]] and fought on the party's side in the Trial of Nine, bringing [[Za'rek]] back up after he went down ([[Session 09]]).

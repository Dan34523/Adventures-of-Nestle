# Companions and Pets

## Joblin

"Joblin the Unemployed Goblin." A goblin captured (bear-hugged into docility by [[Tuff]]) during the [[Wilheim Mine Dungeon]] crawl and adopted by [[Precious]]. Language barrier mostly unresolved, though the party has slowly worked out he understands more than he lets on - he revealed that goblins avoid eating human babies but do eat animal babies, which is how the party connected the dots on [[Braelex]]'s missing child. Turned out to be surprisingly literate himself, or at least Precious relied on him to relay a note. Was seen playing with a pet eggling at the Ball ([[Session 08]]).

## Chance-Martin (the Wolf Cub)

[[Althea]]'s summoned familiar, technically. The party has unofficially renamed it "Chance-Martin" and treats it as a shared pet. Used constantly for scouting ahead, relaying messages, and once as a delivery system for a remotely-cast Blindness/Deafness spell (via bite) during the fight with the [[Wilheim Mine Dungeon|Drider]].

## Adonkey

[[Tuff]]'s donkey. Came bundled with the free pillow-wagon [[Neander Tol]] gave the party as they left [[Wilheim]]. Reliable, uncomplaining, occasionally licks people awake.

## Tesco

[[Tarhun Turneron]]'s pet rat. No further notes yet.

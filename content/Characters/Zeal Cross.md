## Overview
Travelling to [[Wilheim]] for mercenary work when he met [[Morrigan]] on the road. Threatened Castellan [[Neander Tol]] on their first night in town over concerns he'd lie about the reward - not his finest diplomatic moment.

**Backstory:** son of a treasure hunter and member of the **[[Cross Seekers]]**, a tiefling group who used their treasure-hunting wealth to found their own village and trade with neighbouring towns - until poachers and slavers attacked it, slaughtering and enslaving the residents and cutting the horns from tiefling bodies. Zeal escaped, begged neighbouring towns for help that never came, and has wandered Beronay as a mercenary ever since.

Found **Steel Eater**, an acid-bladed sword, in the [[Wilheim Mine Dungeon]] - it became his signature weapon. Later bought **Motion-Weaver**, an enchanted elven blade, from [[Beb Lizardman]] on a 12-month payment plan at 20% interest.

## The Revenge Plot
In [[Ruins of Morrowalk|the Brass Clan's ruined fortress]], Zeal recognised the bandit leader **[[Rodrick Brass]]** as the man responsible for burning the Cross Seekers' village and killing his people. He killed him personally with Motion-Weaver, ending years of unresolved rage.

## Overview
Gravelly-voiced shopkeep of The Purple Dragon, a magic shop with a martial focus. Sold [[Zeal Cross]] the enchanted blade **Motion-Weaver** on a payment plan. Also showed off (but didn't sell, being out of the party's price range) **Flesh-Eater**, a black lava-patterned sword, and the Repulsor Shield / Dragon Scale Shield that caught [[Tuff]]'s eye.

[[Tuff]] returned to buy the Repulsor Shield before the Ball of the Earth Father, and [[Zeal Cross]] cleared the rest of the Motion-Weaver payment plan in the same visit ([[Session 08]]).

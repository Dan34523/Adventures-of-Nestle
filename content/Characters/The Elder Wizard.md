## Overview
Witnessed the failed verification sigil that exposed the fraud behind [[Za'rek's Mother's House]]'s claimed blessing from [[Lolth]]. Did not expose it publicly - instead let quiet rumours spread through the Underdark, valuing the leverage this gave him against a religious institution he despised over any public reckoning.

Facilitated Za'rek's escape through subtle means (guard oversights, unlocked doors) - not out of mercy, but because a living Za'rek remained useful leverage while a dead one was worth nothing. Does not consider Za'rek a protégé; Za'rek does not consider himself indebted to him. Purely transactional, and has made no contact since Za'rek reached the surface - as far as the party knows.

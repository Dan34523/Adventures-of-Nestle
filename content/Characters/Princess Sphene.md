## Overview
The reason for the entire Marriage Mart - her father is throwing a season of balls to find her a match. Chose **[[Apex]]** as her dance partner at the Ball of the Earth Father, to [[Zeal Cross]]'s visible annoyance, then slowly backed away as Apex's improvised solo dance routine turned the room's attention (and her father's fury) on them both. Present for the King's private audience with the party and one of the few people who knows about **[[Prince Zircon]]**'s disappearance.

## Overview
Wandered into the party's camp asking for food, and was given a plate of deer meat by [[Tuff]] with no questions asked. Explained he was looking for his missing baby, taken by goblins weeks earlier when his home was attacked. The party pieced together - via [[Joblin]]'s garbled goblin-culture knowledge - that his "baby" was almost certainly the corrupted **Brass Dragon** hatched and grown by the [[Cult of the Chroma Queen]] in the [[Wilheim Mine Dungeon]], the same one the party was forced to kill.

Continued shadowing the caravan toward [[Solistaire]], to the party's growing unease. Was caught trying to join the caravan from the back in [[Session 05]], and reassigned to a lower-visibility spot.

## The Truth ([[Session 06]] – [[Session 07]])
Followed the party to the [[Ruins of Morrowalk]] disguised in stolen bandit armour, correctly identifying Zeal despite a poor landing. Struck a deal with [[Zeal Cross]]: Braelex would help poison and distribute drugged ale to the bandits (his ale-wagon con got him inside as a "returned prisoner") in exchange for help getting into the goldhouse to question the prisoners about his baby. Zeal agreed, and the plan worked - though Braelex was genuinely credible as a prisoner being escorted back.

In [[Session 07]], confronting Zeal (who reeked of the dragon's blood from **Steel Eater**) about his child, Braelex **transformed into his true brass dragon form**, banished [[Tarhun Turneron]] away as a warning, and demanded answers. Zeal eventually told him the truth: the [[Cult of the Chroma Queen]] had forced them to kill his corrupted child. Rather than attacking, Braelex thanked the party for **sparing his child from a worse fate**.

## Relationship to the Party
Complicated. Wary trust built through the ale-poisoning heist with Zeal; grief and gratitude replacing suspicion once the truth came out. Not currently travelling with the party.

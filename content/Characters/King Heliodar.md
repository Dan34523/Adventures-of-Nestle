## Overview
Aging, handsome ("a silver fox"), father of **[[Princess Sphene]]**, **[[Prince Zircon]]**, and a younger, immature second son, Prince Tanzan. Host of the Marriage Mart - nine balls held in honour of the nine Gods, intended to find Princess Sphene a husband.

Visibly furious after [[Apex]]'s disastrous dance with his daughter at the Ball of the Earth Father ([[Session 08]]).

## The New Quest
Granted the party a private audience at the end of [[Session 08]]. Revealed his first-born son, **[[Prince Zircon]]**, went out with a detachment and never returned, and asked the party to find and bring him back - quietly, since he doesn't want it publicly known the crown needed outside help. Offered a lavish reward (political asylum, holdfasts, commoners, a council seat, bannerman status), all of which [[Za'rek]] talked down to a straightforward, funded search-and-rescue with no strings attached. Only the King, Princess Sphene, the crownsguards present, and **[[Prince Rico LaRoche]]** know of the arrangement - a detail Za'rek is unhappy about but can't undo.

## Overview

Recurring comic-relief NPC with an unshakeable attachment to [[Za'rek]], whom he credits with "saving" him from his own runaway horse (a horse Za'rek had, in fact, spooked while robbing his wagon). Kisses Za'rek's cheeks. A lot.

Backstory: lost his love Annette to his "evil cousin" Armand the Butcher, and is hoping Solistaire - and its Marriage Mart energy - brings him better luck. Rode out to give the caravan fresh croissants on the road ([[Session 05]]).

Moving his bakery to Solistaire; Za'rek has been feeding him rumours (strength from his pastries, reaching the Imperial Guard captain's ear) to help drum up business, and the two are in early talks about Za'rek investing in the shop. Pier has asked Za'rek to find out where **[[Prince Rico LaRoche]]** is staying so he can send free samples.

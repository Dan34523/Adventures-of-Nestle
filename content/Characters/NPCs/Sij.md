## Overview
The last bandit standing after the ambush at the [[Roadside Church of the Silent Lady]], incapacitated by a magic missile "up the butt" (permanent hemorrhoids, allegedly) from [[Za'rek]]. Tried to claim he was a real soldier; [[Althea]] saw through it immediately. Once shown his floating, electrocuted, very dead boss, he talked freely: revealed the clan's name (the [[The Brass Clan|Brass Clan]]), that they're recruiting from surrounding villages, and that their leader is one **[[Rodrick Brass]]**. Oddly progressive for a bandit, per the party's notes, but can't count past his own fingers. Left tied up in Za'rek's grease for the rescued farmfolk to deal with as they please.

## Overview

Appeared to [[Za'rek]] in a dream in [[Session 04]], speaking from within a web-covered vision of the [[Wilheim Mine Dungeon]]. Asked why he ran from her, claimed she'd only ever shown him "the truth," and offered him a place at her side if he returned to the Underdark - the price being **"The Eye," "The Angel Eye," "Her Eye."** Grew increasingly erratic as she spoke, promising rebirth as "a Queen" wrapped in her cocoon, and threatening the slave pits and her eggs when he refused, claiming he doesn't have what she wants.

He woke with a sting in his neck to find a **Drider** - sent by her, presumably - already dragging him out of his tent. The party killed it.

Also territorial about other gods operating in her domain: the Abjuration Crystal in the dungeon existed specifically to block her influence over the [[Cult of the Chroma Queen]]'s ritual, according to [[Harla]].

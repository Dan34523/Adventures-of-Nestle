## Overview
Ran the mercenary recruitment meeting at the start of [[Session 01]], addressing the crowd about the monsters raiding from the old mineshaft and the reward ("as much treasure as you can carry"). Accidentally announced free drinks at [[The Hag's Mole]] were on his office's tab while reading his prepared speech.

Kept the party informed after the night raid (most hired mercenaries died holding the South Gate), arranged Ria Nostakis's invitation to [[Morrigan]] and [[Althea]], and gave the party their pillow-wagon for free when they left for [[Solistaire]] (on the condition they collect it themselves).

## Relationship to the Party
Friendly, a little overwhelmed by his job, generally straight with the party even when the news is bad.

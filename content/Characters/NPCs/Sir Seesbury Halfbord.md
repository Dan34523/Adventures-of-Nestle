## Overview

Met the party at the barracks gatehouse when they arrived in Solistaire escorting Beronay soldier armour looted from bandits. Explained the real threat: coordinated bandit attacks on Beronay soldier platoons along the Aloraxia–Beronay road (the route most nobility use for the Marriage Mart), and that he can't spare his own men, who are needed for Marriage Mart security.

Hired "**Nestle**" (the party) to hunt down the bandit leaders and bring proof, offering monetary reward plus - at [[Precious]]'s insistence - an invitation to one of the nine Marriage Mart balls of their choosing. Recommended they lodge on the barge on the River Solis.

## Overview

Runs Wilheim's apothecary. Awkward and easily flustered around [[Precious]], who he's smitten with. Gave her a free Potion of Healing after the town's first goblin raid, and later lent her the **[[Notable Items and Equipment#Sylvan Sunsmile|Sylvan Sunsmile]]**, a near-immortal flower his family brought from the Feywild generations ago, after she felt too guilty to simply take it. She's promised to return it - and to come back.

## Overview

Local Acolyte under [[Aethswold Telemon]] in Wilheim. Asked [[Morrigan]] and [[Althea]] to recover her master and fellow Acolytes after they were captured in the goblin raid on the town's mine. Offered a blessing of the Martyress to those heading into the dungeon - [[Morrigan]] accepted (a Bless spell after a Celestial prayer), [[Althea]] refused.

Also remembered the man from the [[Sylvan Conclave and Stryxa Centenary#Stryxa Centenary|Stryxa Centenary]] who later turned out to be the High Cultist of the [[Cult of the Chroma Queen]] - he took special interest in both her and [[Harla]] during his visit to Wilheim.

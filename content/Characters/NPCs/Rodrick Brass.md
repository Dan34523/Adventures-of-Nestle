## Overview

Large human bandit leader, identified by written orders found on his lieutenant's body. Recognised by Zeal at the [[Ruins of Morrowalk]] as the man responsible for burning the [[Cross Seekers]]' village and killing/enslaving its people years earlier - including ripping the horns from tiefling victims.

Fought alongside an unnamed robed mage (his second-in-command, also killed in the battle - by Zeal, cut to ribbons with Motion-Weaver mid-duel). Rodrick himself went down fighting [[Tuff]] and [[Tarhun Turneron]] two-on-one before Zeal, empowered by Motion-Weaver, finished him with a flurry of slashes and beheaded him with Steel Eater.

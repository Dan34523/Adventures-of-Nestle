## Overview
Arrived late to the Town Hall meeting in [[Wilheim]], sitting next to [[Za'rek]]. Wears a chainmail poncho by choice. Can officiate weddings, which nobody has needed yet but everyone remembers.

Kicked in doors to rescue sleeping mercenaries the night the goblins attacked. Arm-wrestled [[Precious]] for "her" stolen drink and won - despite an "assist" neither of them asked for (Za'rek used Mage Hand under the table to grab Tuff mid-match).

Traded his old chainmail, shield, and Zeal's spare daggers for a set of Splint Armour from the Dwarven armourer in [[Wilheim]].

Has a donkey named "Adonkey". Came bundled with the free pillow-wagon [[Neander Tol]] gave the party as they left [[Wilheim]]. Reliable, uncomplaining, occasionally licks people awake.

## Overview
Captured in the goblin raid and held in the [[Wilheim Mine Dungeon]] alongside other townsfolk. Visibly nervous around [[Za'rek]] (a drow) - Za'rek noticed and played on it, summoning a light orb to Telemon's face before dismissing it as a joke; Telemon gave a fake laugh about not understanding "Underdark humour."

Rescued by the party and led survivors home with [[Althea]]. Confirmed the identity of the man from the [[Sylvan Conclave and Stryxa Centenary#Stryxa Centenary|Stryxa Centenary]] as a match for the cultist's description, and explained the lore of **[[The Angel Eye|the Angel Eyes]]** and **[[Diopheosis and the Chroma Queen|Diopheosis]]** when [[Morrigan]] and [[Althea]] pressed him about the symbols found in the dungeon. Was stunned to learn the party spared [[Harla]] rather than killing her.

## Overview
Was Archprince and heir to the Imperatrix of Aloraxia as her nephew and closest relation - until the Imperatrix "miraculously" recovered her long-lost son, **Venus LaRoche**, who usurped the title. Rico appears to have accepted the demotion for now, though [[Reginald Applesby]] describes it as "still a touchy subject."

## The Carriage Incident
His procession's carriage broke a wheel on the road to Solistaire; [[Tuff]] and [[Precious]] helped fix it. He then personally stepped out, ordered a guardsman to move **[[Althea]]**'s hair aside, and inspected the red veins on her neck without explanation, before telling her to "enjoy the festivities" and returning to his carriage. Neither Althea nor the party knows why he did this, or whether he recognised her. See [[Mysteries and Plot Threads]].

Later confirmed to be attending the Marriage Mart, seated beside [[King Heliodar]] at the Ball - and is one of only a handful of people who know about **[[Prince Zircon]]**'s disappearance and the party's mission to find him.

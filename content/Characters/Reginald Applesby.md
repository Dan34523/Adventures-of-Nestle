## Overview
Hired [[Zeal Cross]] and [[Apex]] (with near word-for-word identical flattering pitches to each) to scout a few miles ahead of his caravan on the road from [[Wilheim]] to [[Solistaire]]. Generous and easily flattered in turn - the party convinced him to subsidise their entire outfits for the Ball of the Earth Father in exchange for acting as his entourage.

Well-connected: cousin to the **Count of Cuxton**, and a useful source of political gossip, including the succession crisis within [[House LaRoche]].

## Relationship to the Party
Consistently friendly and a reliable source of paid work; the party trusts him more than most employers so far.

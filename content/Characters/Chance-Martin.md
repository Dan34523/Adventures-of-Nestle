## Overview
[[Althea]]'s summoned familiar, technically - a red-furred wolf, currently juvenile. The party has unofficially renamed it "Chance-Martin" and treats it as a shared pet. Used constantly for scouting ahead, relaying messages

## Notes & Secrets
Used as a delivery system for a remotely-cast Blindness/Deafness spell (via bite) during the fight with the [[Session 04|Drider]].

Urinated on a Church of the Martyress Exquirer's shoe at the Ball of the Earth Father, helping cover [[Morrigan]]'s escape ([[Session 08]]).

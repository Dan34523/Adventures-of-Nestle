## Overview
An evasive monk who works the ballroom in [[Solistaire]] ([[Session 08]]). She evidently shares some past encounters with [[Precious]], and is not a fan.

At the Woodland Tourney ([[Session 09]]), she interrupted [[Precious]] during recruitment for the Trial of Nine and ended up reluctantly recruited into the party's team herself. She scouted the accusers' tent alongside [[Za'rek]] under the ruse of a pastry delivery, and fought in the trial, helping take on the ogre.

## Relationship to the Party
[[Za'rek]] quizzed her for gossip at the Ball of the Earth Father and lost interest fast. She and [[Precious]] evidently share some past history, though the details aren't known yet. Recruited reluctantly by [[Precious]] for the Trial of Nine.

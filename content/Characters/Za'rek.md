## Overview
A drow wizard who answered the mercenary muster in [[Wilheim]] ([[Session 01]]) and has been a core member of [[Nestle]] since. Pragmatic and dryly humorous, he handles much of the party's scheming as well as its spells - a covert Mage Hand helped decide the arm-wrestling match between [[Tuff]] and [[Precious]] at [[The Hag's Mole]], and it was his Faerie Fire that caught [[Apex]] mid-burglary there.

He keeps his past to himself, though the party knows he comes from the [[The Underdark|Underdark]] - and that [[Lolth]] has taken an active, unwelcome interest in him.

## Lolth's Interest
In [[Session 04]], Lolth appeared to him in a dream, demanding "The Angel Eye" and offering a place at her side in the Underdark, then threatening him when he refused. He woke to a Drider attack, which the party fought off and killed. He told the others what had happened around the campfire in [[Session 05]]. He has also asked [[Morrigan]] whether her missing eye had ever been called an "Angel Eye"; she denied it.

## Relationship to the Party
Trusted at the party's back, and often the one doing the talking when there is talking to be done - he negotiated extra gold in place of a fifth ball invitation with [[Sir Seesbury Halfbord]] ([[Session 07]]), talked [[King Heliodar]]'s lavish reward down to a straightforward funded search-and-rescue for [[Prince Zircon]] ([[Session 08]]), and ran the pastry-delivery ruse that put the party's tainted pies into the accusers' camp before the Trial of Nine ([[Session 09]]).

## Overview
Argued her way past a suspicious townguard (who assumed she was a "woman of the night" rather than a mercenary, given her minimal coverings) to get into the Town Hall meeting in [[Wilheim]]. Left the party a note: *"I expect bandit heads and a ball invitation by the time I'm back."*

Adopted **Joblin**, a captured goblin from the [[Wilheim Mine Dungeon]], as a mascot/pet - and bought him a +1 magical sword before the Ball of the Earth Father ([[Session 08]]). Formed a fling with [[Edgar Ilwyn]] the apothecary, who lent her the **Sylvan Sunsmile**, a near-immortal Feywild flower heirloom - she's promised to return it.

Revealed at the Ball of the Earth Father ([[Session 08]]) to have a thief's past: a [[Sylvan Conclave]] ambassador she was dancing with recognised her mid-dance and called her out for "getting away with it" - details TBD.

Apparently arranged for **[[Tarhun Turneron]]** to join the bandit-hunting effort - though nobody's actually confirmed it was her doing the hiring (see [[Mysteries and Plot Threads]]).

Fought for [[Reginald Applesby]] in the Trial of Nine ([[Session 09]]), reluctantly recruiting **[[Ruko]]** - the evasive monk she evidently shares some past encounters with - and taking on the ogre alongside [[Zeal Cross]] and Ruko. Her offer to deliver [[Pier]]'s tarts became the pastry-delivery ruse that got the party's tainted pies into the accusers' camp ahead of the fight.

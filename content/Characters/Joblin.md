## Overview
"Joblin the Unemployed Goblin." 
A goblin captured (bear-hugged into docility by [[Tuff]]) during the [[Wilheim Mine Dungeon]] crawl and adopted by [[Precious]]. She later bought him a +1 magical sword before the Ball of the Earth Father ([[Session 08]]). 

Language barrier mostly unresolved, though the party has slowly worked out he understands more than he lets on - he revealed that goblins avoid eating human babies but do eat animal babies.

## Relationship to the Party
[[Precious]] is the main communicator with Joblin, as she is the owner of the [[Amulet of Common Tongue]]

## Overview
First met the party as a **burglar**, not an ally - he snuck into the party's room at [[The Hag's Mole]] under Invisibility to rob their newly-won treasure, and was only caught when [[Za'rek]]'s Faerie Fire dispelled the spell. Introduced himself awkwardly as "Apex" on the spot and talked his way out of a beating.

Later hired properly (alongside [[Zeal Cross]]) by [[Reginald Applesby]] to scout ahead of the caravan to [[Solistaire]]. Was reassigned to guard the back of the caravan after Applesby's people noticed a stranger ([[Braelex]]) joining from behind.

Runs a side hustle in stolen jewellery with his [[Yakuza Family]], an elderly criminal contact ("Granny") and her network of child pickpockets, aiming to sell through the Silver Street black market in Solistaire. 

Danced with **[[Princess Sphene]]** at the Ball ([[Session 08]]) and comprehensively ruined it by improvising his own "cultural" dance mid-song, embarrassing the party and visibly angering **[[King Heliodar]]**.

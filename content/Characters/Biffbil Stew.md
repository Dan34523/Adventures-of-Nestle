## Overview
Found hiding in a bush by the [[Roadside Church of the Silent Lady]] after the ambush - he'd guided a group of Beronay soldiers to the **[[Ruins of Morrowalk]]** the day before, and hid when the [[The Brass Clan|Brass Clan]] bandits (disguised in that same armour) arrived and started killing people. Interrogated and recruited to guide the party to the ruins in turn, which he did without complaint, choosing not to question the party's habits along the way (including trying to roll him down a hill).

He was killed by Apex off-screen.

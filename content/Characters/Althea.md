## Overview
Sent by the **Church of the Silent Lady** to help the townsfolk of [[Wilheim]]; the Castellan mistook her for the town's own combat medic assignment.

Refused Ria Nostakis's offered blessing before the dungeon crawl, unlike [[Morrigan]], who accepted.

## The Missing Memory
Althea has **no memories from before she joined the Church of the Silent Lady roughly six months ago** - startling, given she appears to be around 25. She left the church almost immediately after joining. Neither she nor the party knows why the gap exists.

## The LaRoche Incident
On the road to [[Solistaire]], a well-dressed man stepping out of a broken-down Imperial carriage - believed to be **[[Prince Rico LaRoche]]** - ordered a guardsman to move Althea's hair aside so he could inspect the red veins on her neck, then told her to "enjoy the festivities" before leaving. She claims not to know him. The party isn't convinced he doesn't know *her*. See [[Mysteries and Plot Threads]].

## Notes & Secrets
- [[Morrigan]] detected a strong magical aura from an object in Althea's bag during Detect Magic - and never told her.
- Not much is known yet about what she carries or why the church sent her specifically.

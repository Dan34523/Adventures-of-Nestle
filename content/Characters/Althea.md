## Overview
Sent by the **Church of the Silent Lady** to help the townsfolk of [[Wilheim]]; the Castellan mistook her for the town's own combat medic assignment, which she leaned into. Doesn't fight like a typical holy caster - her power is visibly corrupted eldritch magic: Eldritch Blast that disintegrates targets outright, and spells that inflict brain hemorrhages or necrotic rot.

Refused Ria Nostakis's offered blessing before the dungeon crawl, unlike [[Morrigan]], who accepted.

## Appearance
Althea stands 5'7", with pale skin and a slight sickly appearance. She seems to improve and grow healthier with each level up, and is currently starting to look healthier in weight. Her hair is golden blonde, wavy, and around shoulder length, with red tints; her eyes are golden with red irises. Crimson veins reach up to her chin - it is unclear where else on her body they appear. She is usually accompanied by her familiar, a red-furred juvenile wolf the party unofficially calls [[Chance-Martin]].

## The Missing Memory
Althea has **no memories from before she joined the Church of the Silent Lady roughly six months ago** - startling, given she appears to be around 25. She left the church almost immediately after joining. Neither she nor the party knows why the gap exists.

## The LaRoche Incident
On the road to [[Solistaire]], a well-dressed man stepping out of a broken-down Imperial carriage - believed to be **[[Prince Rico LaRoche]]** - ordered a guardsman to move Althea's hair aside so he could inspect the red veins on her neck, then told her to "enjoy the festivities" before leaving. She claims not to know him. The party isn't convinced he doesn't know *her*. See [[Mysteries and Plot Threads]].

## The Ball of the Earth Father
At the Ball ([[Session 08]]), [[Prince Rico LaRoche]]'s servant asked Althea to dance with the Prince; she declined, getting out of the situation by dancing with the servant instead.

The same night, when a Church of the Martyress Exquirer tried to detain [[Morrigan]], Althea helped cover the escape by having her wolf [[Chance-Martin]] urinate on the Exquirer's shoe.

## The Blinding Light
In [[Session 09]], a blinding light burst out of Althea and vanished almost immediately, leaving both her and [[Tuff]] unconscious. She had no pulse but kept breathing. [[Precious]] could tell the cause was not natural, while [[Za'rek]] could not identify it. The condition was temporary and she is confirmed alive, though nothing else about the light has been explained.

## Notes & Secrets
- [[Morrigan]] detected a strong magical aura from an object in Althea's bag during Detect Magic - and never told her.
- Not much is known yet about what she carries or why the church sent her specifically.
- Searched bookshops and libraries in [[Solistaire]] for texts on the Silent Lady, and picked up a book written by an ancient priest of the Silent Lady ([[Session 08]]).

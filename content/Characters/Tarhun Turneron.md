## Overview
Found naked and dirty outside [[The Hag's Mole|the party's barge room]] in Solistaire, delivered along with [[Joblin]] and the wolf pup by the Bargekeep. Claims he was hired by "a tall woman in Monk's robes wearing hand-wraps" - the party assumed this meant [[Precious]], though nobody's confirmed it with her directly (see [[Mysteries and Plot Threads]]).

Hired in exchange for a share of treasure and a mud bath, Tarhun joined the hunt for **[[The Brass Clan]]**. Fights with a warhammer and raw physicality - crushed skulls outright in the Church of the Silent Lady ambush and the Ruins of Morrowalk battle, tanked a Thunderwave from the enemy mage, and finished off multiple bandits solo. Effectively adventuring with the party full-time as of [[Session 06]].

Briefly banished by [[Braelex]] (in dragon form) during the confrontation over his missing child in [[Session 07]], as a warning to get the party to tell him the truth.

Has a pet rat, Tesco.

At the Woodland Tourney ([[Session 09]]) he lost a mud-wrestling match to [[Siggins McDiggins]] - but was convinced by [[Morrigan]] to join the party's team for the Trial of Nine. Tesco escaped during the tournament and refused to be caught until [[Za'rek]] tempted the rat with a pie.

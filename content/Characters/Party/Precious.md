## Overview

Argued her way past a suspicious townguard (who assumed she was a "woman of the night" rather than a mercenary, given her minimal coverings) to get into the Town Hall meeting in [[Wilheim]]. Turns out to be surprisingly, secretly literate - the party only found out when she left them a note: _"I expect bandit heads and a ball invitation by the time I'm back."_

Adopted **Joblin**, a captured goblin from the [[Wilheim Mine Dungeon]], as a mascot/pet. Formed a fling with [[Edgar Ilwyn]] the apothecary, who lent her the **Sylvan Sunsmile**, a near-immortal Feywild flower heirloom - she's promised to return it.

Revealed at the Ball of the Earth Father ([[Session 08]]) to have a thief's past: a Sylvan Conclave ambassador she was dancing with recognised her mid-dance and called her out for "getting away with it" - details TBD.

Apparently arranged for **[[Tarhun Turneron]]** to join the bandit-hunting effort - though nobody's actually confirmed it was her doing the hiring (see [[Mysteries and Plot Threads]]).

## Overview

Arrived at [[Wilheim]] answering word of a lucrative mercenary job, meeting [[Zeal Cross]] on the road. Guards at the gate assumed she was a holy person because of her priestly robes and veil - an assumption that's stuck ever since.

Raised from infancy in the **[[Church of the Martyress]]** after her parents dropped her off as a baby. During a ritual to "promote" her from Acolyte, the church took one of her eyes - she now wears a veiled headband to cover it. The torture and the theft disillusioned her, and she left. When [[Za'rek]] asked whether her missing eye had ever been called an "Angel Eye," she denied it (see [[The Angel Eye]]).

Awakened an **Innate Divine Sorcery** during the fight with the [[Wilheim Mine Dungeon|Drider]] - sprouted heavenly wings and unleashed psychic attacks. Also throws Sorcerous Bursts (fire, thunder, acid, poison depending on the moment) and can cast Bless and Shield.

## Notes & Secrets

- Cast Detect Magic during the loot-counting in [[The Hag's Mole]] and sensed a stronger magic coming from an object in **[[Althea]]'s bag** - and chose not to mention it. Nobody else knows this yet.
- Accepted Ria Nostakis's blessing (a Bless spell) before the dungeon crawl; [[Althea]] refused hers.
- Has no memory of ever using the term "Angel Eye" for her missing eye, but was visibly uncomfortable when asked.

# The Angel Eye

The campaign's central dangling plot thread. Treat this page as a living document - update it every time a new clue surfaces.

## What We Know
- **Angel Eyes** were magical superweapons created during **[[Diopheosis and the Chroma Queen|Diopheosis]]**, allowing mortals to wield the power of the Gods against other Gods.
- Most were destroyed in the **Wizard War** that followed Diopheosis, per [[Aethswold Telemon]] - but he isn't certain none survive.
- Documents found in the [[Wilheim Mine Dungeon]] study showed correspondence between an unmarked sender and **Bor'Xhas** of [[The Underdark|Tier Breche]], discussing a ritual to *create* an Angel Eye using sacrifices, and whether it could be used to return the **Chroma Queen** to life.
- **[[Lolth]]** appeared to [[Za'rek]] in a dream demanding "The Eye," "The Angel Eye," "Her Eye" as the price for his return to the Underdark - implying she believes he has one, or access to one.
- When Za'rek asked **[[Morrigan]]** whether her missing eye (taken by the [[Church of the Martyress]] during her Acolyte promotion ritual) had ever been called an Angel Eye, she **denied it** - visibly uncomfortable.

## Open Questions
- Is Morrigan's missing eye actually an Angel Eye, whether or not she knows it?
- Does Za'rek possess or carry something that qualifies, without realising it? (His violet-tinted magic, a side effect of the failed verification sigil, is the obvious candidate.)
- Is the [[Cult of the Chroma Queen]] still trying to build/find one after the loss of their leader and ritual site?
- Does the strange, more-powerful magic object [[Morrigan]] secretly detected in [[Althea]]'s bag have anything to do with this?

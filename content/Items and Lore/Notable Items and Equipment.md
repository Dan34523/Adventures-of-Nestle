## Steel Eater
**Owner:** [[Zeal Cross]]. An acid-bladed sword found in the [[Wilheim Mine Dungeon]]. Used to free enslaved prisoners in the [[Ruins of Morrowalk]] goldhouse and to kill the corrupted Brass Dragon (its acid blade melted through bone and brain). Zeal's signature weapon before Motion-Weaver arrived.

## Motion-Weaver
**Owner:** [[Zeal Cross]]. An enchanted curved elven blade, previously traded in by an indebted [[Sylvan Conclave and Stryxa Centenary|Sylvan Conclave]] noble. Bought from [[Beb Lizardman]] on a 12-month payment plan at 20% interest. Speeds its wielder up with each successful strike - used to devastating effect against the Brass Clan mage and **[[Rodrick Brass]]**. Paid off prior to the Ball of the Earth Father

## Bracers of Well-Being
**Owner:** [[Morrigan]]. Found in the locked armoury cabinet in the [[Wilheim Mine Dungeon]]. Amplifies her divine healing.

## The Thunder Clappers
**Owner:** [[Precious]]. Found alongside the Bracers of Well-Being. Empowers his Divine Smites with thunder damage - used to devastating effect against the corrupted Brass Dragon and later Rodrick Brass's mage.

## Wand of Detect Magic
**Owner:** [[Za'rek]]. Bought from [[Beb Lizardman]]'s shop, The Purple Dragon, funded by selling off loot from the Wilheim dungeon. Used to sense magical presences (including the mysterious hooded Drow at the [[Ruins of Morrowalk]]).

## Barrier Coronet
**Owner:** [[Precious]]. Bought at the Invulnerable Vagrant in [[Solistaire]] with rubies looted from the Wilheim dungeon.

## Sylvan Sunsmile
**Owner:** Borrowed by [[Precious]] from [[Edgar Ilwyn]]. A near-immortal Feywild flower with a technicolour stem and glass petals, brought by Edgar's family from the Feywild after the Wizard War. Precious has promised to return it.

## Bag of Holding
**Owner:** The party (carried by [[Morrigan]]). Used to store payment and, memorably, to smuggle [[Morrigan]] herself out of a locked room at the Ball of the Earth Father.

## Wand of Webs
**Owner**: [[Morrigan]]. Purchased prior to the Ball of the Earth Father. Tested on Zeal. Allows the user to cast the spell Web without using spell slots.

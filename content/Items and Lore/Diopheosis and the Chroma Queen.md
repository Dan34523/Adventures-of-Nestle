## Diopheosis
An ancient divine war, referenced in Underdark symbolism (Infernal words for Rebirth, Excess, Evolution; Celestial words for Sacrifice, Purification, Angel) found scrawled in blood on the support beams of the [[Wilheim Mine Dungeon]]. During Diopheosis, mortals were empowered with **[[The Angel Eye|Angel Eyes]]** to fight against the Gods themselves.

## The Wizard War
The conflict that followed Diopheosis. Most Angel Eyes were destroyed during it, according to [[Aethswold Telemon]] - though he isn't sure the destruction was total. [[Edgar Ilwyn]]'s family fled the Feywild to the material plane during this same war, generations ago, bringing the **Sylvan Sunsmile** with them.

## The Chroma Queen
A fallen god, cast into the Abyss during Diopheosis, associated with chromatic dragons (implied to be linked to **Tiamat**). Worshipped by the **[[Cult of the Chroma Queen]]**, who believe that freeing her would end all the world's suffering. [[Lolth]] is known to be territorial about other gods - even fallen ones - operating within her domain, which is why the cult's ritual site needed the Abjuration Crystal to mask it from her.

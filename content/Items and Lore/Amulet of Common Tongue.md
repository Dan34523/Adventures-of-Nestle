## Description & Properties
Moulded from silver into the shape of a two-headed bird with a sapphire eye and a ruby eye. It can be activated by touching the eyes of the bird. Once activated, you can understand the literal meaning of any common language that you can hear. While the literal meaning is translated, it will not allow you to understand coded language or any cultural or dialectic influences.

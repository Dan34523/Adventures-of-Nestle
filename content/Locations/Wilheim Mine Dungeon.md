## Description
A disused mineshaft that became the base for a goblin tribe working with the [[Cult of the Chroma Queen]]. Entrance guarded by goblins around a campfire; inside were prisoner cells (holding [[Aethswold Telemon]] and other townsfolk), a First Cavern goblin camp, a Main Corridor with four branching rooms, and a Final Cavern where the ritual took place.

### Main Corridor Rooms
- **Crystal Room** - a purple **Abjuration Crystal** (blocking [[Lolth]]'s influence) guarding four treasure chests behind a magic barrier.
- **Armoury** - cabinets of ritual sickles, robes, masks, and goblin weapons; one magically locked cabinet held **[[Notable Items and Equipment#Bracers of Well-Being|The Bracers of Well-Being]]**, an enchanted Great Axe, and **[[Notable Items and Equipment#The Thunder Clappers|The Thunder Clappers]]**.
- **Study** - papers on **[[Diopheosis and the Chroma Queen|Diopheosis]]**, the fallen Chroma Queen, and **[[The Angel Eye]]**; correspondence between an unmarked sender and a "Bor'Xhas" of [[The Underdark|Tier Breche]] about creating an Angel Eye through sacrifice; a Scroll of Absorb Elements and a Scroll of Revivify.

### The Final Cavern
Site of the ritual: a blood sigil, four fire pits, and the hatching/corruption of a **Brass Dragon** egg using blood and molten gold, followed by a forced Dominate Monster. The party defeated the High Cultist and [[Harla]] here, killing the enthralled young dragon in the process - later revealed to be **[[Braelex]]**'s child.

## Description
Wilheim's tavern, where the party first stayed after being hired. Descended into a "medieval frat party" the first night - troubadours, overflowing drinks, an Orc arm-wrestling contest between [[Tuff]] and [[Precious]] (won by Tuff, with covert help from [[Za'rek]]'s Mage Hand). Burned down that same night when monsters attacked the town; the Orc barmaid was killed. Rebuilt by the time the party returned from the dungeon in [[Session 03]].

Also the site of [[Apex]]'s failed attempt to rob the party of their dungeon loot while invisible - foiled by Za'rek's Faerie Fire.

## Description
A small roadside church passed twice by the party - once peacefully on the way to Solistaire, once under attack by disguised **[[The Brass Clan]]** bandits on the way back, who were rounding up and executing farmfolk over hidden gold. The party (plus [[Tarhun Turneron]]) broke up the attack, rescued the farmfolk, and interrogated the survivors ([[Sij]], [[Biffbil Stew]]) here before pushing on to the [[Ruins of Morrowalk]].

Note: this is a *different* institution from [[Althea]]'s Church of the Silent Lady posting - same faith, different chapter.

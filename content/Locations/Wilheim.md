## Description

Once a prosperous trade town, fallen into poverty by the time the party arrives - monsters from a disused mineshaft (see [[Wilheim Mine Dungeon]]) have been raiding it and the surrounding trade routes at night. The party was recruited here as part of a large mercenary muster; most of the other hires died defending the South Gate during the night raid in [[Session 01]].

## Notable Locations

- **[[The Hag's Mole]]** - the town's tavern/inn, burned down and later rebuilt.
- **Town Hall** - site of the recruitment meeting.
- **Market Square** - became a tent village for refugees; site of the goblin raid battle.
- **The [[Church of the Martyress|local church]]** - led by [[Aethswold Telemon]] and [[Ria Nostakis]], with a secret storage hatch behind the altar.
- **Pier's Patisserie** - [[Pier]]'s bakery wagon, since relocated to [[Solistaire]].
- **Edgar Ilwyn's Apothecary** - run by [[Edgar Ilwyn]].
- **The Blacksmith & The Armourer** - Orc-run smithy and Dwarven armoury, both used by the party for trades and repairs.

# Regions and Nations

## Kingdom of Beronay

Ruled by **[[King Heliodar]]** from the capital, **[[Solistaire]]**. Home to [[Wilheim]] and the surrounding countryside. Currently hosting the Marriage Mart to find **[[Princess Sphene]]** a husband, and dealing with a coordinated bandit problem ([[The Brass Clan]]) on its main southern trade road.

## Empire of Aloraxia

Home of **[[House LaRoche]]**, the Imperial Family, ruled by an Imperatrix. Capital: **Ankh'Orel**. Recently went through a **succession crisis**: Prince Rico LaRoche was Archprince and heir until the Imperatrix's "miraculously" recovered long-lost son, Venus LaRoche, usurped the title. The Empire has a documented pattern of scapegoating tieflings and "demon worshippers" during instability - the reason [[Zeal Cross|Zeal]]'s Tiefling refugee acquaintances fled toward Beronay.

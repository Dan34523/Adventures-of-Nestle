## Description
Walled capital of Beronay, currently hosting the **Marriage Mart** - a season of nine balls, one per God, meant to find **[[Princess Sphene]]** a husband. Weapons must be surrendered and magic items are inspected (and judged) on entry. Laws forbid drawing weapons unprovoked or misusing magic.

## Notable Locations
- **The Barracks** - where [[Sir Seesbury Halfbord]] hired the party to hunt [[The Brass Clan]].
- **The Barge (River Solis)** - the party's lodging, recommended by Seesbury; run by an Orc Bargekeep.
- **Wizback Wang's Store of Wonders** - high-end magic shop, out of the party's budget.
- **The Invulnerable Vagrant** - newer magic shop; [[Precious]] sold rubies here for the **Barrier Coronet**.
- **The Scale Mail** - armourer; sold [[Zeal Cross]] vambraces bearing the Royal House of Larimar's sigil.
- **The Purple Dragon** - martial magic shop run by [[Beb Lizardman]]; sold Zeal **Motion-Weaver** and Za'rek his **Wand of Detect Magic**.
- **Silver Street** - black market district, [[Apex]]'s intended fence for stolen jewellery.
- **The Count of Cuxton's residence** - host site for the Ball of the Earth Father, attended as [[Reginald Applesby]]'s entourage.
- **Castle Larimar** - the seat of the King of Beronay. Located on the **Shining Hill**

## Description
A ruined fortress used as the main hideout of the ~40-strong **Brass Clan**, discovered with [[Biffbil Stew]]'s guidance. Contained an alewagon, a "goldhouse" holding prisoners (including a captured [[Zeal Cross]]), and a bunkhouse. Destroyed in the climactic battle of [[Session 06]]: the alewagon was poisoned and then blown up (Eldritch Blast + a fire Sorcerous Burst), the goldhouse prisoners freed, and both **[[Rodrick Brass]]** and his bandit mage killed in the ensuing courtyard fight.

An upstairs room in the far two-storey building held the bandits' stolen loot and armoury - and a **mysterious hooded female Drow**, who vanished by scroll-teleport after clocking Zeal and Althea but pointedly avoiding Za'rek's eyes, promising "her mistress would find this most interesting." See [[Mysteries and Plot Threads]].

# Session 03 - New Employer, New Enemies

## Quick Recap

The party returns to Wilheim, confronts the church, meets a would-be thief, and takes a new job escorting a caravan to [[Solistaire]] - which is ambushed by bandits on the road.

## Key Events

- Return to [[Wilheim]] with the dungeon loot. [[Neander Tol]] welcomes them back and gives Precious's pet goblin his name: **Joblin**.
- [[Morrigan]], [[Althea]], and [[Zeal Cross]] confront **[[Aethswold Telemon]]** and **[[Ria Nostakis]]** about Harla, while Zeal secretly searches a hidden hatch behind the altar (finds a holy book, ornaments, a potion, and a bloody whip).
- **[[Apex]]** ("the Badest Chief") sneaks into the rebuilt [[The Hag's Mole]] invisible to rob the party - caught by Za'rek's Faerie Fire, laughed off, sent packing.
- **[[Reginald Applesby]]** hires [[Zeal Cross]] and [[Apex]] to scout ahead of his caravan to [[Solistaire]] for the Marriage Mart.
- On the road: an armed patrol turns out to be **[[The Brass Clan|bandits]]** in stolen Beronay armour. The party wins the fight decisively.

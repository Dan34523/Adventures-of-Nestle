## Key Events
- [[Reginald Applesby]] goes to gather the carriage. [[Apex]] does not join.
- A blinding light emerges from [[Althea]], and immediately vanishes, leaving [[Tuff]] and herself unconscious. She doesn't have a pulse, but still manages to keep breathing. [[Precious]] can discern that the cause was not natural, however [[Za'rek]] completely fails to identify what the reason is. The party continues to Cuxton Manor, where [[Reginald Applesby]] withdraws to the drawing room.
- [[Baron Harkwyn]] comes in to the drawing room after [[Reginald Applesby]] talks with the party. The Baron slaps Applesby. He takes off his mask, and reveals his massive purple bruise on his face. Furious at the stain on his honour due to [[Tuff]]'s allegations against him, he demands a Trial of Nine.
- The party agrees to fight for [[Reginald Applesby]]. They make their way to the Woodland Tourney. The party attempts to recruit 4 additional members. 
- [[Morrigan]] finds [[Tarhun Turneron]] in a mud wrestling tournament, against an incredibly built gnome. In the first charge, the gnome slides under Tarhun's legs and pins him. The second goes even worse, and grabs Tarhun who immediately slips and falls to the floor. He manages to claw back a round, winning round 3. Fourth round, the gnome grabs his legs and immediately falls to the floor, making him the winner. [[Morrigan]] convinces [[Tarhun Turneron]] to join, and [[Za'rek]] recruits the gnome - [[Siggins McDiggins]].
- [[Precious]] approaches a rogue-ish figure with an eye-patch. Turns out he's already bought, by the Baron. He works for the Don of Dons and Criminals. After failing to recruit him, [[Precious]] gets interrupted by an evasive woman, revealed to be **[[Ruko]]**. She recruits her reluctantly.
- [[Zeal Cross]] is still looking, and suddenly hears a debilitating shriek out of nowhere, audible only to him, bringing him to his knees. He recovers and continues looking, and finds a ranger, the same ranger that was at the [[Ruins of Morrowalk]] - Zero, who is now recruited to the party.
- Reginald needs something to settle the nerves, and Precious goes to the pastry wagon to get a tart for him, and it's [[Pier]]! She attempts to get information on who will fight with the Baron. Pier informs her on the tent that the accusers are fighting at. She offers to deliver the tarts to them for Pier.
- [[Za'rek]] finds [[Morrigan]] and [[Tarhun Turneron]], still trying to catch Tesco, Tarhun's pet rat, after the wrestling match, but the rat keeps eluding them. Za'rek takes a pie and manages to get Tesco to bite it. He and Ruko then go to gather information on the accusers under the ruse of pastry delivery, finding the tent and handing over the tainted pies. Inside, they spot two knights and a squire: Sir Simon Starkheight, his squire, and a second knight wielding a crab harpoon (name unknown).
- It's time for the fight, and the party instructs [[Reginald Applesby]] to stay back while they deal with the other fighters, eight in all, including a large ogre. One of the knights on the accusers' side, Sir Simon Starkheight, is missing, taken out of the fight by the tainted pies; a squire is sent in his place, wielding a legendary sword far too large for him to carry.
- A servant approaches the announcer from the accusers' half, whispers something in his ear, and a pouch is seen changing hands, probably a bribe for the invisible attacker. The announcer then introduces the combatants, along with the rules of the fight.
- The fight begins, with the party advancing on their enemies. Blows are exchanged; [[Za'rek]] casts sleep, taking out numerous enemies at once, while [[Zeal Cross|Zeal]], Ruko, and [[Precious]] take on the ogre. Za'rek is downed by an enemy's Lightning Bolt but brought back up by [[Morrigan]]. An enemy druid wild-shapes into a lioness and attacks [[Tarhun Turneron|Tarhun]]. Over time, the still-awake enemies are knocked unconscious. Suddenly, an invisible figure attacks, revealing themselves to be the accusers' ninth member. As soon as he appears, the announcer denounces the accusers and declares [[Reginald Applesby]] innocent of all charges. Just as fast, the invisible figure disappears again.

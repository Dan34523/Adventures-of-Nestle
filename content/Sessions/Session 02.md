# Session 02 - Into the Mine

## Quick Recap

The party gears up, meets **[[Ria Nostakis]]**, and heads into the **[[Wilheim Mine Dungeon]]**, ultimately uncovering - and stopping - a ritual by the **[[Cult of the Chroma Queen]]** to corrupt a Brass Dragon.

## Key Events

- [[Morrigan]] and [[Althea]] meet **[[Ria Nostakis]]**, who asks them to rescue her master **[[Aethswold Telemon]]** and fellow Acolytes. [[Morrigan]] accepts a Bless blessing; [[Althea]] refuses hers.
- Prep in town: gear trades at the blacksmith and armourer; [[Precious]] and [[Edgar Ilwyn]] grow closer.
- Party enters via the Goatpass, ambushes the goblin guards at the entrance, and frees the prisoners including **[[Aethswold Telemon]]**.
- Explore the dungeon's Main Corridor: the Crystal Room (Abjuration Crystal + 4 chests), the Armoury (finds **[[Notable Items and Equipment#Bracers of Well-Being|Bracers of Well-Being]]** and **[[Notable Items and Equipment#The Thunder Clappers|The Thunder Clappers]]**), and the Study (Diopheosis lore, correspondence about the **[[The Angel Eye|Angel Eye]]**).
- Confront the High Cultist and **[[Harla]]** in the Final Cavern as they ritually hatch and corrupt a Brass Dragon (later revealed to be [[Braelex]]'s child). Kill the High Cultist and the dragon; Harla surrenders.
- **The party spares Harla** after a debate, learning her backstory and the Abjuration Crystal's true purpose (blocking [[Lolth]]). She helps deactivate it, takes half the remaining treasure, and leaves.

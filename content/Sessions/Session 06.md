# Session 06 - The Brass Clan Falls

## Quick Recap

The party meets **[[Tarhun Turneron]]**, rescues a church under attack, and wipes out the [[The Brass Clan|Brass Clan]] at the [[Ruins of Morrowalk]] - where Zeal finally gets his revenge.

## Key Events

- **[[Tarhun Turneron]]**, a homeless Bronze Dragonborn veteran, joins the party - hired, he claims, by "a tall woman in Monk's robes" (assumed to be [[Precious]], who is off-page for most of this session).
- The party finds the **[[Roadside Church of the Silent Lady]]** under attack by disguised bandits and breaks it up, rescuing farmfolk and capturing informants **[[Sij]]** and **[[Biffbil Stew]]**.
- Guided by Biffbil Stew to the **[[Ruins of Morrowalk]]**. [[Zeal Cross]] sneaks in (helped, unexpectedly, by a disguised **[[Braelex]]**) to poison the bandits' ale supply and free the goldhouse prisoners.
- Full assault on the ruins: **[[Rodrick Brass]]** and his mage are killed. Zeal recognises Rodrick as the man who destroyed his childhood village and the [[Cross Seekers]], and finishes him personally with Motion-Weaver.
- A hooded female Drow is spotted looting the upstairs armoury before teleporting away, mentioning an unnamed "mistress."

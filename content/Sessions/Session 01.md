# Session 01 - The Hag's Mole Burns

## Quick Recap

The party is recruited as mercenaries in [[Wilheim]] to clear monsters out of an old mineshaft. Their first night in town, the town is attacked, the tavern burns, and they fight off a goblin raiding party in the Market Square.

## Key Events

- Recruitment meeting at the Town Hall, hosted by Castellan **[[Neander Tol]]**. The party (and everyone else) is offered "as much treasure as they can carry" to clear the raiders.
- Night at **[[The Hag's Mole]]**: an arm-wrestling match between [[Tuff]] and [[Precious]] (Tuff wins, with covert help from [[Za'rek]]'s Mage Hand).
- The town is attacked in the night. The tavern burns; the barmaid is killed. The party helps evacuate and fight in the Market Square against **7 goblins and a Goblin Boss**, defeating them.
- [[Precious]] rescues the wounded guard **[[Rob]]**; [[Althea]] heals him.
- [[Za'rek]] pranks [[Pier]] the baker (steals tarts while "helping" with his runaway horse) and steals from Pier's wagon.

## NPCs Encountered

- [[Neander Tol]] · [[Rob]] · [[Pier]] · [[Edgar Ilwyn]] _(Precious's apothecary encounter)_

## Locations Visited

- [[Wilheim]] · [[The Hag's Mole]]

## Loot & Rewards

- Goblin loot: gold teeth, silver earrings, a set of keys (Za'rek and Precious).
- A Potion of Healing, given to Precious by Edgar Ilwyn.

# Session 05 - The Road to Solistaire

## Quick Recap

Backstories around the campfire, an uncomfortable run-in with Aloraxian royalty, and the party finally reaches [[Solistaire]].

## Key Events

- [[Pier]] catches up with the caravan to deliver croissants and gossip about the **Marriage Mart** - nine balls for the nine Gods, to find **[[Princess Sphene]]** a husband.
- Fireside backstories: [[Morrigan]] reveals her history with the [[Church of the Martyress]] and her missing eye; [[Za'rek]] reveals the Lolth dream and Drider attack; [[Zeal Cross]] shares the fall of the [[Cross Seekers]]; **[[Althea]] reveals she has no memories from before joining the Church of the Silent Lady six months ago**, despite appearing to be around 25.
- A House LaRoche carriage breaks down on the road; [[Tuff]] and [[Precious]] help fix it. A mysterious noble (believed to be **[[Prince Rico LaRoche]]**) inspects the red veins on [[Althea]]'s neck and tells her to "enjoy the festivities."
- The party arrives at [[Solistaire]], passes gatehouse inspection, and is hired by **[[Sir Seesbury Halfbord]]** to hunt down the bandit leaders responsible for the road attacks - the **[[The Brass Clan|Brass Clan]]** - in exchange for a reward and a Marriage Mart ball invitation.
- **[[Reginald Applesby]]** hires the party as his entourage for the Ball of the Earth Father, in exchange for paying for their outfits.
- Shopping spree: [[Precious]] buys the **Barrier Coronet**; [[Zeal Cross]] buys vambraces and puts a deposit down on **Motion-Weaver**; [[Za'rek]] buys a **Wand of Detect Magic**.

# Session 07 - Braelex's Truth

## Quick Recap

Braelex reveals his true nature and gets closure; the party collects their bandit-hunting reward and gets fitted for the Ball of the Earth Father.

## Key Events

- **[[Braelex]] transforms into a Brass Dragon**, confronting [[Zeal Cross]] about the smell of his baby's blood on Steel Eater. Banishes [[Tarhun Turneron]] away as a warning during the confrontation.
- Zeal comes clean: the **[[Cult of the Chroma Queen]]** forced the party to kill Braelex's corrupted, already-dying child. Rather than attacking, **Braelex thanks the party for sparing his child a worse fate.**
- The party returns to [[Solistaire]] to collect their reward from [[Sir Seesbury Halfbord]] - only 4 ball invitations for the group, so [[Za'rek]] negotiates extra gold instead of a fifth.
- The party arranges to attend the Ball as **[[Reginald Applesby]]**'s entourage instead. Shopping trip for masks and outfits, fully paid for by Applesby. Za'rek picks a lilac snake-head mask and a gold suit.

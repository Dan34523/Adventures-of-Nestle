# Session 04 - The Drider

## Quick Recap

Camp life on the road: hunting, gossip, a strange stranger, and a Lolth-sent nightmare that turns into a real attack.

## Key Events

- Camp set up at a cliffside clearing; loot from the bandit ambush divided.
- Hunting trip (Zeal, Tuff, Morrigan) with a dwarven hunter nets a deer and an elk. [[Apex]] recruits a desperate tiefling refugee family for his side hustle.
- **[[Braelex]]** wanders into camp looking for his lost baby, taken by goblins weeks earlier. The party connects the dots (via [[Companions and Pets#Joblin|Joblin]]'s testimony) that his baby may have been the corrupted Brass Dragon they killed in [[Session 02]] - though they don't tell him yet.
- **[[Lolth]]** appears to [[Za'rek]] in a dream, demanding "The Angel Eye" as the price of his return to the Underdark, and threatening him when he refuses.
- Za'rek wakes to a real **Drider** attack. The whole party fights it off and kills it.

## NPCs Encountered

## Locations Visited

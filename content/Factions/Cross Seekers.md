## Overview

A group of tiefling treasure hunters, including Zeal's father, who used their earnings to establish their own village and trade with neighbouring towns. The village was destroyed by poachers and slavers - residents slaughtered or enslaved, with tieflings' horns cut from their bodies as trophies or trade goods. Zeal escaped, found no help from neighbouring settlements, and has been a wandering mercenary ever since.

The clan's destroyer, **[[Rodrick Brass]]**, was tracked down and killed by Zeal in [[Session 06]].

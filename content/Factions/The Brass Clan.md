## Overview

A newly-formed bandit clan (~40 members) recruiting from surrounding villages, stealing Beronay soldier uniforms and armour to impersonate real soldiers, then using that disguise to rob and terrorise travellers and villages along the Aloraxia–Beronay road - including the [[Roadside Church of the Silent Lady]]. Coordinated enough to draw the attention of **[[Sir Seesbury Halfbord]]**, who hired the party to hunt them down.

Answered to an unnamed "business partner" (per [[Sij]]'s testimony) whose identity was never established before the clan's leadership was wiped out.

## Notable Members

- **[[Rodrick Brass]]** - leader; personally responsible for destroying [[Zeal Cross]]'s childhood village years earlier. Killed by Zeal.
- **The Brass Clan Mage** _(unnamed)_ - Rodrick's second-in-command; killed by Zeal.
- **[[Sij]]** - low-ranking, spared and left with the farmfolk.

## Relationship to the Party

Destroyed as of [[Session 06]]. The "business partner" thread is unresolved.

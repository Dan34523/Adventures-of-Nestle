## Overview

A minor drow House whose recent rise in status was built on a **fraudulent claim of [[Lolth]]'s blessing**. When Za'rek - the matriarch's bastard son, cast out at birth - accidentally exposed the fraud via a verification sigil, the House had every reason to want him silenced permanently, especially once **[[The Elder Wizard]]** let rumours of the fraud spread. Attempts were made to quietly contain the situation before Za'rek fled to the surface.

Status of the House's suspicions or search for Za'rek since his escape: **unknown**. See [[Mysteries and Plot Threads]] for whether the hooded Drow woman spotted at the [[Ruins of Morrowalk]] is connected.

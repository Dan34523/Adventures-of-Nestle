# Sylvan Conclave & Stryxa Centenary

Two separate minor factions, filed together for now since both have only appeared in passing.

## Sylvan Conclave
A fey-aligned political body; sent a representative and ambassador to the Marriage Mart balls in [[Solistaire]]. **[[Precious]]** danced with their ambassador at the Ball of the Martyress, who recognised her mid-dance from an apparent past theft ("you thieving bitch..."). An indebted Sylvan Conclave noble is also the prior owner of **Motion-Weaver**, the blade [[Zeal Cross]] bought from [[Beb Lizardman]].

## Stryxa Centenary
The Magic Academy of [[Regions and Nations#Empire of Aloraxia|Aloraxia]]. Source of "the High Cultist" who radicalised [[Harla]] and led the [[Cult of the Chroma Queen]]'s ritual in the [[Wilheim Mine Dungeon]]. Also produced the student who enchanted one of the scimitars in [[Beb Lizardman]]'s shop (not purchased).

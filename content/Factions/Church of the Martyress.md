## Overview
A church whose initiation rites for Acolytes are, in practice, brutal: ritualistic torture and self-flagellation intended to "strengthen faith," which in at least two known cases (**[[Morrigan]]** and **[[Harla]]**) produced the opposite effect. Morrigan lost an eye to the church during her own promotion ritual before leaving; Harla was radicalised by a cultist recruiter while enduring the same process and ultimately betrayed the church entirely.

The Wilheim chapter is led by **[[Aethswold Telemon]]**, with **[[Ria Nostakis]]** as the sole surviving local Acolyte after the events of [[Session 01]]–[[Session 02]].

## Relationship to the Party
Complicated. The party rescued Telemon and other captured townsfolk, but also has direct, personal reasons ([[Morrigan]]'s history) to distrust the institution itself.

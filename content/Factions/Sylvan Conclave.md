## Overview
A fey-aligned political body; sent a representative and ambassador to the Marriage Mart balls in [[Solistaire]]. Their ambassador, **Lord Glydin Seransandros**, danced with **[[Precious]]** at the Ball of the Earth Father and recognised her mid-dance from an apparent past theft ("you thieving bitch...") - implications unresolved.

An indebted Sylvan Conclave noble is also the prior owner of **Motion-Weaver**, the blade [[Zeal Cross]] bought from [[Beb Lizardman]].

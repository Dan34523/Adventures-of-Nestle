## Overview

Worships **the Chroma Queen** - a fallen god associated with Tiamat, cast into the Abyss during [[Diopheosis and the Chroma Queen|Diopheosis]]. Recruits from the disillusioned and desperate: their key operative, **[[Harla]]**, was a tortured Acolyte of the [[Church of the Martyress]] who was radicalised by "the High Cultist," a visitor from the **[[Sylvan Conclave and Stryxa Centenary|Stryxa Centenary]]**.

Orchestrated the goblin raids on [[Wilheim]] as cover to acquire sacrificial resources, and performed a ritual in the [[Wilheim Mine Dungeon]] that hatched and corrupted a Brass Dragon egg (using blood magic and molten gold) into a controlled weapon - the egg turned out to belong to **[[Braelex]]**.

## Notable Members

- **The High Cultist** - led the ritual, killed by the party in [[Session 02]].
- **[[Harla]]** - spared, defected in practice, left the region.
- **A hooded female Drow** - spotted at the [[Ruins of Morrowalk]] reporting to an unknown "mistress." Possibly cult-affiliated, possibly connected to [[Za'rek's Mother's House]] or [[Lolth]] instead. Unconfirmed.

## Relationship to the Party

Actively hostile until its leadership was killed; ongoing loose thread via the hooded Drow woman.

## Overview
The Imperial Family of Aloraxia. **Prince Rico LaRoche** was Archprince and heir until the Imperatrix "miraculously" recovered a long-lost son, **Venus LaRoche**, who usurped the title, leaving the Imperial court divided into bickering factions. Rico appears to have accepted the demotion, for now.

House LaRoche's procession crossed paths with the party on the road to [[Solistaire]] ([[Session 05]]) when a carriage wheel broke - during which **[[Prince Rico LaRoche]]** took an unexplained, personal interest in **[[Althea]]**.

## Relationship to the Party
Cordial but watchful. Rico is now aware of the party's discreet mission for [[King Heliodar]] to find [[Prince Zircon]].
